Grilled Cheese Sandwich

A simple, comforting, American, midday meal

Making a Grilled Cheese is as simple as it looks!

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2-3 slices of cheese
  • Butter

Steps:

  1. Butter a single side for both of the slices of bread
  2. Start heating a pan
  3. Put a single slice of the bread onto the pan, butter facing down
  4. Put the slices of cheese on top
  5. Place the other slice of bread on the pan, butter facing up
  6. Let it cook until the bottom is a golden brownish color
  7. Flip!
  8. Cook the other side again until its the same color and the cheese melts
  9. Take it off an enjoy!
