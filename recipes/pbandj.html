Peanut Butter & Jelly Sandwich

Another classic American lunch time staple

Easy to make, and delicious!

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices of bread
  • Peanut Butter
  • Jelly/Jam

Steps:

  1. Place the 2 slices of bread infront of you
  2. Spread peanut butter on one slice
  3. Spread the jelly on the other
  4. Put the slices together with the peanut butter and jelly touching
