Ajvar

A popular Balkan spread, especially in Serbia and North Macedonia

To make Ajvar Spread, you need to throw together a bunch of red bell peppers, some eggplant, sunflower oil, salt, white or wine vinegar, and maybe some sugar.

Ingredients:

  • Red Bell Peppers - 5.5 lbs
  • Eggplant - 1.5 lbs
  • Sunflower Oil - 3/4 cup
  • Salt - 1.5 tablespoons
  • White or Wine Vinegar - 1.5 tablespoons
  • Sugar (optional) - 1.5 tablespoons

Steps:

  1. Bake the peppers and eggplants until they char
  2. Put the roasted ingredients into a closed bag for the steam to loosen their skin
  3. Strip the charred skin, open, and scrape the vegetables
  4. Drain the peeled flesh overnight
  5. Mince the flesh until it is finely chopped
  6. Put it into a pot, add half the oil, cook on low and stir
  7. Pour the remaining oil over the next 2 hours
  8. Stir in salt, sugar, and vinegar in the last 15 minutes
  9. Pour the hot ajvar into jars and seal tightly
